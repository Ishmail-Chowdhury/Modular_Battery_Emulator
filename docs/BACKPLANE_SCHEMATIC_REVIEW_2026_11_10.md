# Pisa Backplane V2 schematic review

The build uses two backplanes and eight CVEs per backplane for the voltage-only CMU bench setup. The baseline `2 Backplanes` sheet of the combined BOM selects 44 fitted parts per board and leaves the I1 current-card socket empty. The earlier standalone BOM priced the fully populated original and included I1. The mux revision adds U8, C9 and R27–R29 per board. The schematic uses the combined selection, including M1, T1, J12 and the auxiliary relays, and keeps the two externally sourced TE part numbers. It uses manufacturer part numbers and confirmed BOM selections without treating the RFQ leads as purchases. [Original Pisa release](https://github.com/batterylabunipi/Modular_Battery_Emulator/tree/a0d4499b8810e432f862b3f266d868523a92c2fc) · [Pisa paper, §§3–4](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/12/5/1232)

## Findings in the original design

- **The original relay option allowed a supply short.** Fitting R8 with R9/R13, R11 with R10/R12, R15 with R14/R16, or R18 with R17/R19 could bridge +48 V to GND even with the relay off. The revised schematic and layout remove R8/R11/R15/R18 and their copper stubs. J13 pins 1, 3, 5 and 7 still carry +48 V with the selected 0 Ω population. Pins 2, 4, 6 and 8 switch to GND through their relays.
- **Relay LED current limiting belongs on the controller side.** M1.16/.18/.20/.22 go straight to the relay anodes and M1.24 is their separate `SWgnd` return. The SUPSiC reference circuit requires 5–30 mA of LED drive. The backplane has no series resistor or driver for those pins. Verify the drive current, return and turn-off state if J13 is used. The relay branches are independent of the CVE voltage path. [SUPSiC reference circuit and operating conditions](https://uploadcdn.oneyac.com/attachments/files/brand_pdf/supsic/19/06/GAQY215G1S.pdf)
- **Pisa provided optional series bridges.** R1–R7 join adjacent cell outputs within a backplane. Pisa marked these positions DNM in the released CAD and BOM. The paper describes eight series-connected CVEs per module but gives no assembly instruction showing whether its prototype used the pads or external links. The current 16-cell build requires R1–R7 to be fitted on both backplanes. [Pisa paper, §3](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/12/5/1232)
- **The original backplane shared MISO at M1.33 and M1.34.** Multiple CVEs fitted to the same bus put their MAX14850 push-pull outputs on that same net. Deselecting a card can put the ADC's SDO in high impedance while its isolator output still drives the host bus. The new U8 mux selects the eight individual S3 traces before they reach M1.33. [MAX14850 pinout and output stage](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/MAX14850.pdf) · [MCP3564R SDO behavior, §3.3.3](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/MCP3561R-Data-Sheet-200006391B_.pdf)
- **J5 and J8 carry power as well as board I/O.** J5.8 is +48 V and J5.1 is GND. J8.1 is +48 V and J8.8 is GND. Their other pins route to M1 board I/O. The CVE SPI lines go through M1. For the reviewed 3.3 V CVEs, M1.39 supplies the host side of their isolators and M1.40 is its signal return. M1.39 retains the original CVE net name `5V_in`. The selected SPI connection uses M1.37 clock, M1.35 MOSI and M1.33 muxed MISO.
- **The archived purchasing exports lose distinctions.** The old CSV groups R8/R11/R15/R18 (0805) with R1–R7 (1812), groups all Aux headers as 1×2 and lists two logos where native CAD has three. Aux6/Aux12 are 1×10, Aux19/Aux20 are 1×3 and Aux21/Aux22 are 1×1. The C1–C8/I1 datasheet description says 0.050 inch. TE specifies 0.100 inch for signal contacts and 0.300 inch for power contacts. U5–U7 are printed graphics and H1–H12 are board mounting holes. Use the revised schematic and selected BOM for assembly planning. [TE hybrid connector drawing](https://www.te.com/en/product-1761426-3.html)

## Wiring two backplanes to the CMU

Each CVE output connector uses pin 1 for negative and pin 2 for positive. Fit all seven FOJAN FRC1812P000 TS jumpers per board (JLCPCB C2907555). The part is rated 2 A and below 50 mΩ. The native 1812 lands are intended for hand soldering. Check the first assembly and measure tap voltage drop under the intended load. [FOJAN FRC datasheet](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/lcsc/2411071710_FOJAN-FRC0805F9100TS_C2930211.pdf)

| Link on each PCB | Nodes joined by the fitted PCB link | CMU tap on lower board | CMU tap on upper board |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | J2.2 to J3.1 | 1 | 9 |
| R2 | J3.2 to J4.1 | 2 | 10 |
| R3 | J4.2 to J6.1 | 3 | 11 |
| R4 | J6.2 to J7.1 | 4 | 12 |
| R5 | J7.2 to J9.1 | 5 | 13 |
| R6 | J9.2 to J10.1 | 6 | 14 |
| R7 | J10.2 to J11.1 | 7 | 15 |

Connect lower J2.1 to tap 0 and upper J11.2 to tap 16. Join lower J11.2 to upper J2.1 for tap 8. B8 is the only external series bridge. Take one CMU sense lead from each node B0–B16. For interior taps use the positive terminal of the preceding cell. Attach the B8 sense lead to the bridge junction. Keep the taps on the floating cell stack. The wiring schedule gives logical CMU inputs. Physical CMU connector pin numbers require the matching board schematic.

## FPGA readback through the card edge

Fit CVE R40, R42 and R44. Each CVE sends its ADC reply through U3 S3 and a dedicated backplane trace to U8. The FPGA selects the slot at M1 pins 26, 28 and 30 and reads MISO from M1 pin 33. See the [on-board readback and M1 pin map](PISA_ONBOARD_READBACK.md) and [readback BOM additions](Pisa_readback_BOM_additions.csv).

## Verification

KiCad 10.0.8 verified 514 surviving pin assignments across 166 nets. Schematic and PCB net membership match. Schematic ERC found zero errors and 351 inherited warnings. PCB DRC after a test zone refill found zero errors, zero unconnected pads and 126 warnings, down from 146. The eight-way terminals still need a sample fit in the documented row.

The R1–R7 population update retained all 514 pin assignments and 166 nets. The rerun found zero electrical or layout errors, zero unconnected pads, 351 ERC warnings, and 126 DRC warnings. The seven junctions on each board and the 17 logical CMU taps were checked against the KiCad netlist.

## Two-layer readback layout — 2026-11-11

The backplane is now routed on F.Cu and B.Cu. It retains the single U8 mux, all existing component positions and pin assignments, the card-edge S3 readback paths and the M1 interface. The buffer-per-slot concept was not adopted, so the selected readback BOM is unchanged. Seven short sections each of Bus1, Bus3 and Bus5 were rerouted to let the individual returns cross the original shared bus area. The floating cell-output copper and R1–R7 connections are unchanged.

The saved board has zero DRC errors, zero unconnected items, 126 existing DRC warnings and 80 existing schematic parity warnings, with no parity errors. The board outline and all 87 footprints / 698 physical pad instances match the previous mux revision. Details and the remaining bench checks are in [PISA_ONBOARD_READBACK.md](PISA_ONBOARD_READBACK.md).
