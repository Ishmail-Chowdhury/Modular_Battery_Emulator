## Layout revision

- KiCad 10.0.8 reports **0 errors and 0 unconnected items** after copper refill and reopening the saved board. The full report contains 139 inherited library and silkscreen warnings. The revised board preserves all 358 original mapped pad connections.
- Removed the zero-length outline segment, assigned priorities to the overlapping ground pours and corrected the ground connections at J5 and the ADC divider positions. Removed three dangling trace stubs. The external outline dimensions and card-edge profile are preserved.
- Reworked the U5/U9 PowerPAD thermal vias. Each now has 18 regular 0.33 mm thermal vias under the exposed pad plus the four original outer thermal holes. The revised spacing resolves the 42 drill-spacing warnings without lowering the KiCad clearance requirement. Check the soldered PowerPAD and loaded thermal behavior on the first card. ## Changes in the schematic

- Added the selected manufacturer, exact `PN`, `JLCPCB` identifier and data link to 80 existing references. Short on-sheet values show device families where the order code would crowd the drawing. New library symbols for U1, U4, U6 and U12 preserve existing PCB pad numbers. The native board and assigned land patterns are preserved for the separate layout review.
- Put the C17477 zero-ohm jumper at R17. The native root sheet and PCB tie U8 feedback to isolated ground through R17. R4 is DNM. Populating both would short the 1.225 V reference. The archived BOM and copied purchasing row had R4 and R17 reversed. The jumper count stays at six per card.
- Added the previously selected RB1 as a manual 22 Ω, 3 W load from isolated `+5V` to isolated `GND`. C5 pads 1 and 2 are the specified attachment points. The legacy symbol has no PCB footprint. Keep RB1 separate from the PCB assembly export if KiCad later offers to add it to the board. The five removable C5305 shunts are a manual supply item documented on the root sheet.
- Marked the U6 land pattern **HOLD** and the three 62 Ω replacements **conditional**. Updated the three 120 Ω values to their actual 0.02% tolerance. Marked U1 Trim intentionally open and updated the FPGA-side supply note.

## One-card checks before committing to sixteen

1. Resolve U6 land pattern and the outstanding PCB rule checks. Verify the received converter’s pin diagram against the datasheet and verify U1 isolation and rail polarity with the card unpowered.
2. Fit RB1. Check U1 startup and ripple, 3.3 V stability, RB1 temperature and the 2.5 V and 1.225 V references before connecting the CMU. For the 16-card plan, RB1 alone adds about 18 W dissipated across the cards.
3. With J1 host-side 3.3 V and its separate return, scope CS, clock, MOSI and MISO. Confirm one DAC write and a valid ADC reply, then read and calibrate CH0 at several points spanning the intended range. Use a DMM at `C+` relative to isolated GND and compare loaded and unloaded results. Measure a representative diagnostic or balancing load before depending on the upper-voltage margin.
4. For multiple cards, provide a separate MISO return per selected path or an external mux/buffer arrangement. The MAX14850 MISO output is push-pull. Its host-side output does not follow the ADC’s high-impedance deselect state. [MAX14850 unidirectional output stage](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/MAX14850.pdf)

For backplane assembly, fit the 0402 0 Ω links at R40, R42 and R44 and leave J2, J3 and J4 unbridged. R44 routes the host-side ADC reply to U3 contact S3. See the [on-board readback and M1 pin map](PISA_ONBOARD_READBACK.md).
